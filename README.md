## Part I · The Negative-Mass Mirage: The First Closed Path

### I.1 Thought Experiment: The Two Marbles in the Vacuum Chamber

Imagine a vacuum chamber containing two marbles. The first marble has the ordinary properties of matter: positive inertial mass, positive passive gravitational mass, positive active gravitational mass. The second marble, through some hypothetical mechanism, has negative inertial mass, negative passive gravitational mass, but unspecified active gravitational mass. We release the marbles at rest, separated by one meter.

In the ordinary case (both positive), the marbles fall together under their mutual gravitational attraction, accelerate toward each other, and collide. In the doubly-negative case (everything reversed), Bondi's 1957 analysis shows: the negative marble experiences a gravitational pull from the positive marble (because its passive gravitational mass is negative, the force flips sign, becoming repulsive). The positive marble experiences the ordinary attractive pull from the negative marble's active gravitational mass (whatever its sign). If the active gravitational masses are *both* equal to their inertial masses with the same sign convention (as required by the equivalence principle), then: the positive marble is pulled toward the negative one (attraction toward negative active mass = repulsion, so it accelerates *away*). The negative marble is repelled from the positive one (its negative passive mass converts attractive force to repulsion, so it also accelerates *away from* the positive marble — but with negative mass, F = ma gives acceleration *toward* the positive marble, since dividing by negative inertia flips the direction).

The result: the positive marble flees away, and the negative marble chases it. Both marbles accelerate continuously in the same direction. Energy and momentum are conserved exactly, because the negative-mass kinetic energy decreases as the negative marble accelerates, exactly compensating the positive-mass kinetic-energy increase. The center of energy-momentum is stationary throughout the chase. No conservation law is violated. The equivalence principle is preserved (each marble feels its own local gravitational acceleration as the equivalent of an inertial acceleration in a fictitious frame). General relativity is internally consistent with this scenario.

But the runaway pair has never been observed in 67 years of cosmic-ray detectors, accelerator experiments, or astronomical surveys. The Bondi-Sachs asymptotic-mass formalism (Bondi, van der Burg, Metzner 1962; Sachs 1962) further demonstrates that any localized, gravitationally-radiating system loses positive Bondi mass at null infinity — the gravitational radiation is always *positive*-energy.
